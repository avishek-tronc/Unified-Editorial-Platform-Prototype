Choose your MSC file: it is shown here, organized by section, so you can review it first. Then select the journal and run the integrity check. The evaluation covers:

  • Accuracy and completeness of the content
  • Typos and language
  • Plagiarism (text similarity)
  • Graphs, figures and equations
  • Table data
  • Technical conformance and identity/authorship integrity
  • Scope fit against the journal portfolio, with best-fit journal or transfer recommendation
